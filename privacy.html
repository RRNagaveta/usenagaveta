Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

NaGaveta is an app developed by RR Serviços to help you keep and organise your personal documents and to remember important dates (expiry dates, reminders and medicine times).

This policy explains, clearly and honestly, how NaGaveta handles (or rather, does not handle) your data.

Summary in one sentence

NaGaveta works locally: your documents, your data and your PIN stay on your device. No server receives your documents; the app requires no sign-up, does not track you and does not sync anything. The only sending that happens is what you yourself decide to do — when you share a file, when you export a backup or when you open the gov.br signing service. The text reading feature (OCR) works on the device itself.

Three honest caveats, detailed below: (a) if iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, the app data may be part of the system backup, performed by Apple; (b) Google's text recognition component may send technical diagnostic data; (c) on Android, the app may send an anonymous usage count — whether it was opened and whether it reached 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 stored items —, with no data about you or your documents, and you can turn it off (item 6).

1. What data NaGaveta keeps and where it stays

All your data stays on your device. NaGaveta has no server and no cloud storage for your data — there is no service of ours for your documents to go to. (Our only service is the one that adds up the anonymous count in item 6, which receives nothing from your documents.)

Only the following is kept on your device, in a private area that only the app itself can access:

  • The documents, photos and PDFs you capture or import;
  • The titles, categories, expiry dates, reminders and medicines you enter;
  • Your access PIN and your preferences.

The app does not send, copy or sync any of this outside your device. NaGaveta has no automatic backup of its own and does not sync between devices.

System backup (iPhone) — please read: if iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, NaGaveta data may be part of the device backup, as happens with other apps, according to your iOS settings. That backup is an operating-system feature, performed by Apple into your iCloud account — it is not NaGaveta syncing, and we still have no access whatsoever to your data. You control it in Settings → your name → iCloud.

Protection of stored files: on iPhone, the app declares the highest level of iOS Data Protection. While the device is locked, the system keeps NaGaveta files encrypted at rest. The app does not add encryption of its own on top of that.

Important consequence: since the data stays only on your device and we have no copy of it, if you uninstall the app or lose the device, the data cannot be recovered by us, because we never had access to it.

2. Automatic text reading (OCR)

NaGaveta can read the text of your documents to help identify information such as dates. This reading is done entirely inside your device, using Google's on-device text recognition technology (ML Kit).

The image of your document is not sent to any server for this reading.

Honest statement: ML Kit is a component provided by Google. Although the content of your document is not sent, that component may: (a) download the text recognition model once; and (b) transmit to Google technical diagnostic data and an installation identifier, not linked to your identity. According to the privacy manifests published by the component itself, this data is not used for cross-app tracking or advertising. This is part of Google's component, not a feature created by NaGaveta, and it never includes your documents. Apart from it, the only transmission is the anonymous usage count described in item 6. To understand this handling, consult Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Internet connection

NaGaveta does not make network requests to send your documents or your personal data. In normal use, the app works offline. The only request the app makes on its own is the anonymous usage count in item 6 (on Android), which contains no data of yours.

The only interactions with the "outside world" are started by you:

  • Sharing a document: when you use the share button, your phone's own system opens the options (WhatsApp, email, etc.) and the file goes wherever **you choose**. From the moment you share it, the file is handled by the destination app you selected.
  • Signing at gov.br: when you tap this option, NaGaveta opens the address of the official government signing service (gov.br is the Brazilian government's official digital signature service) in your phone's browser. The app only opens the address; your document does not go with it — the whole interaction happens between you and the gov.br website.
  • Exporting a backup: see item 4 below.

In every case, sending any information to third parties is your choice.

4. Vault backup — read carefully

Since your documents stay only on your device, changing phone or losing it would mean losing everything. That is why NaGaveta lets you make a copy.

When you tap "Back up now", the app assembles a single file containing all your documents, reminders and medicines and hands that file over to you through your phone's own sharing. NaGaveta does not choose the destination and does not send anything on its own: you are the one who decides where the file goes.

What this means in practice, said plainly:

  • From the moment you send that file somewhere — email, Google Drive, WhatsApp, computer, USB stick — it is no longer protected by the app and comes to depend on the security of the destination you chose.
  • The backup file is not encrypted and is not protected by your PIN. Anyone who has the file can open its content. Keep it with the same care you would keep your paper documents.
  • We do not have and will never have a copy of that file. If you lose it, there is no way to recover it with us.

When restoring a backup, the app reads the file inside your device and puts the documents back into the vault. Nothing is sent out during this process.

5. Receiving documents from other apps

You can send a file to NaGaveta from another app (an email attachment, a PDF from WhatsApp, a file from Drive), using that app's "Share" option and choosing NaGaveta.

In that case the file is copied into the vault, on your device, and comes to follow all the rules of this policy. NaGaveta does not access the content of the other app — it only receives the file you sent.

6. Data collection and tracking

NaGaveta does not collect personal data about you and does not track you. We do not use:

  • Third-party usage analysis tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase);
  • Crash reporting;
  • Adverts;
  • Login or account creation;
  • Any behaviour tracking.

The app uses third-party technical libraries only to function (such as taking a photo, opening a PDF, showing notifications and reading text). ML Kit, described in item 2, transmits to Google technical diagnostics and an unlinked installation identifier, and nothing else. We at NaGaveta neither receive nor consult that data.

Purchases: the subscription and Premium are paid through the store itself (Google Play or App Store). We do not receive card or payment data.

Anonymous usage count (Android)

So that we know whether people who install NaGaveta actually start using it, the Android app may send, at most once each, only these notices:

  • the app was opened for the first time;
  • you reached 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 stored items.

After 5 items, no further count is sent — whether you have 6 or 5,000.

What each notice contains: only the name of the notice (for example, "reached 3 items") and whether it came from the store version or a test version. It contains no identifier of you, your device or the installation (not even the advertising ID), no version, language, country or device model, and nothing about your items: name, type, category, content, recognised text (OCR), dates, reminders, medicines, searches or files. The app does not read any of that to build the notice — only the quantity.

Where it goes: to a service run by RR Serviços on Google Apps Script, which only adds up the notices: it keeps the total of each one and the total per day. No notice is stored individually, and the service does not receive or store IP addresses. As with any internet connection, the IP address and the technical connection data reach the infrastructure of Google, which operates the service; we have no access to them. Daily totals are kept for up to 400 days; overall totals, for as long as the service exists.

What it is for: knowing, in aggregate numbers, how many installations actually start using the app — including to evaluate how NaGaveta is promoted. These numbers identify no one and are neither sold nor shared.

People who already used NaGaveta before this feature existed send no notice. To turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage count. When off, nothing else is sent. On iPhone, this count does not exist.

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to alert you at the time of your medicines and reminders.
  • Exact alarms: so that the alert arrives at the set time, and not late.
  • Ignore battery optimisation: requested only once, so that Android does not postpone the alerts when the device is idle.
  • Rescheduling after restarting the device: so that your reminders keep working after the phone is switched off and on again.
  • Vibration: for the vibration of notifications.
  • Internet access: the app declares this permission, and it is only fair to explain why. It is required by two components: Google's ML Kit, which may download the text reading model once (item 2), and the opening of the browser in the gov.br shortcut (item 3). **NaGaveta does not use this permission to send your documents or your data anywhere** — there is no server of ours to send them to. The anonymous count in item 6 also uses the internet, and contains no data of yours.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices / local network: only when you use sending between nearby devices, to find the other device and transfer the documents directly between the two, without going through the internet.
  • Microphone: only when you tap to search by voice. Your speech is recognised on the device itself and is neither recorded nor sent.

NaGaveta does not ask for camera permission or for access to your photos/files. The camera is opened by your phone's own camera app, and file selection uses the system picker. The app also does not access location or contacts.

8. Your access PIN

The PIN you create to protect the app is kept only on your device and never in readable text. We keep only a transformed and irreversible version of it (a "hash"), used solely to check whether the PIN you typed is correct. The original PIN is never stored or sent anywhere.

Honest statement: this protection keeps the PIN out of plain text and off any server, but it is not bank-level encryption. The PIN serves as a layer of privacy for ordinary day-to-day use.

9. Children's data

NaGaveta is not directed at children and does not intentionally collect data from minors. Since the app does not collect personal data from any user — the anonymous count in item 6 identifies no one —, no children's information is gathered by us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. When that happens, the "last updated" date at the top will be changed. We recommend reviewing this page from time to time.
